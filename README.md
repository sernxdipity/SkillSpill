# Skillspill 🌱
### Connect. Share. Support.

**Sime Darby Property University Hackathon 2026 | PEOPLE Category**

Skillspill is a mobile-first community engagement platform designed to strengthen neighbourhood connections by encouraging residents to interact, exchange skills, and support one another.

Our vision is to create inclusive and connected townships where residents can contribute their knowledge, time, and abilities to build stronger communities.

## The Problem

Despite living close to one another, residents may face challenges connecting with neighbours, discovering community activities, accessing affordable learning opportunities, and finding everyday assistance.

Skillspill aims to address these challenges through a convenient, community-driven digital platform.

## Key Features

### 1. Community & Groups
- Discover and join neighbourhood interest groups.
- Participate in community activities and discussions.
- Meet residents with similar interests.

### 2. Skill Share
- Discover workshops and learning opportunities.
- Share knowledge and teach skills.
- Connect with residents interested in learning.

### 3. Neighbour Help
- Request assistance with simple everyday tasks.
- Offer help to other residents.
- Encourage mutual support within the neighbourhood.

## Additional Features

- **Time-Credit System:** A proposed reward mechanism for exchanging verified teaching and assistance hours.
- **Community Good Deeds:** Celebrates residents' positive contributions through achievements, comments, and celebration reactions.
- **Weekly Quests:** Encourages residents to participate in collective community challenges.
- **Multilingual Support:** Language preferences designed to make the platform more inclusive.
- **Resident Profiles:** Personalised interests, skills, and participation history.
